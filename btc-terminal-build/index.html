Global news intelligence

Institutional money flows

ETF net flows, issuer holdings, corporate treasury purchases and government reserves.

Integration slots: Farside Investors / issuer disclosures / Bitcoin Treasuries / public filings. ETF flow history and holdings are not populated from the free RSS feeds. Add licensed provider/API connections before treating these as live figures.

Derivatives & positioning

Open interest · funding · long/short ratio · liquidations · options expiry · implied volatility · CME basis/gaps.

Integration slots: CoinGlass, exchange APIs (Binance, OKX, Deribit) and CME data. API access, regional availability and redistribution terms vary. Do not infer liquidation levels from incomplete data.

On-chain analytics

Whale flows · exchange balances · miner flows · active addresses · MVRV · SOPR · NUPL · holder cohorts.

Integration slots: Glassnode, CryptoQuant, Coin Metrics or mempool.space. Many advanced metrics require paid subscriptions. Raw blockchain transactions are not equivalent to verified exchange flows.

Global macro & cross-asset markets

DXY · US 2Y/10Y yields · S&P 500 · Nasdaq · VIX · gold · silver · crude · global liquidity · India macro.

Integration slots: FRED, Stooq, Nasdaq Data Link, Trading Economics or a licensed market-data provider. Some indices and real-time exchange quotes require paid permissions.

Bitcoin network & mining

Hash rate · difficulty · mempool · fee market · block interval · miner economics · hashprice · halving.

Public network data can be sourced from mempool.space and Blockchain.com; mining profitability and hashprice need specialist data providers.

Regulation, government & geopolitical risk

SEC · CFTC · US legislation · RBI · Indian taxation · sanctions · tariffs · wars · elections · banking stress.

The news feed currently polls selected public RSS sources. Add official source feeds for each jurisdiction and geopolitical news providers for broader coverage. Headlines are not a substitute for reading official notices.

Sentiment & crowd positioning

Fear & Greed · Google Trends · social sentiment · influencer statements · exchange positioning.

Integration slots: Alternative.me Fear & Greed API, Google Trends and licensed social/listening data. Social sentiment can be manipulated and should be treated as a noisy indicator.
BTC Intelligence Terminal · Gemini cloud AI free-tier limits apply. Data can be delayed, incomplete or revised. AI analysis is informational, not financial advice. Verify major events with primary sources.
